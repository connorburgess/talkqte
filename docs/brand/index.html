Shared System Typography & signature cue anatomy

Type Scale and Cue Anatomy

Shared across all three directions: upright Manrope Variable for fast glance legibility, IBM Plex Mono for provenance and status, and one consistent four-part cue structure from notebook to companion to talkqte.com.

Role Spec Specimen
Site Hero Display Manrope 500 · 54px / 1.06 Stay grounded. Respond in the moment.
Notebook Cue (Standard / Large) Manrope 600 · 22px / 28px Earlier, legal said review takes two days.
Companion Cue (Standard / Large) Manrope 600 · 17px / 21px Will the draft be ready Wednesday?
Source Quote (Notebook / Companion) Manrope 400 · 14px / 13px “Can we commit to Friday?”
Provenance, Status & Labels IBM Plex Mono 500 · 11px THIS CONVERSATION · 04:08 · JORDAN · IN SOURCE, NOT YET RELEASED
Four-part cue anatomy (app, companion, and website) No Save action · No countdown numbers · No coloured left border on default cues
01 · Label Line

Plain kind + quiet marker

Uppercase IBM Plex Mono states what the cue is (Context, You could ask, Just said, @ Asked you) with dismiss quietly in the corner.

02 · Statement

Complete at a glance

Upright Manrope at 22px/28px (notebook) or 17px/21px (companion). Every word is readable immediately without clicking or hovering.

03 · Time Line / Hold

Quiet bar, never digits

A 3px proportional line shows remaining unread time and doubles as the 44px Hold control. Direct-address cues stay without a countdown.

04 · Quoted Source

Inspectable provenance

Monospace source line above the verbatim quote from the conversation or selected note. Nothing invented, nothing saved automatically.
