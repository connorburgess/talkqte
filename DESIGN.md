# TalkQTE interface

The first interface is a working notebook, with the warmth and disciplined rhythm of a community radio programme log. This is an implementation direction, not a final brand identity.

- Deep evergreen navigation, warm paper work areas, dark ink, and muted terracotta for the primary demo action.
- System sans-serif type for native familiarity; tabular numerals only for time and counts.
- A stable sidebar, searchable meeting list, and spacious note editor. The active question appears in a dedicated companion area or a separate desktop window.
- The signature interaction is a question's shrinking time bar. Expiry is quiet and carries no penalty; save and dismiss remain explicit buttons.
- The desktop companion anchors at the bottom-right of the notebook display. Its compact bar shows explicit demo status, pause/resume, and hide; a question expands upward while the bottom edge stays put. It returns to the bar on pause, save, dismissal, or expiry.
- Rounded controls with consistent stroke icons; separators organize dense content without enclosing every paragraph in a card.
- Hover, focus, loading, error, empty, paused, and ended states are part of the interface. Reduced motion removes entrance motion and progress transitions.
- All sample content is labeled demo. No fake capture status, model readiness, integrations, or persistence guarantees.
