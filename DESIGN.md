# TalkQTE interface

The interface is a quiet meeting companion with a friendly notebook. Rounded forms, readable type, and generous actions make a question approachable without adding pressure to answer. This is an implementation direction, not a final brand identity.

- Deep evergreen navigation, warm paper work areas, dark ink, and muted terracotta for the primary demo action.
- Rounded system type where the platform supplies it, with native sans-serif fallbacks. Body uses 16px text; a cue uses a 20px heading (26px at the larger size) with 15px source excerpts. Tabular numerals are reserved for elapsed time and counts.
- One sidebar combines Notebook navigation, a searchable meeting list with compact rows, and Settings beside a spacious note editor. Optional first-run setup and meeting focus expand on request; notebook tabs use an underline and the editor sits on the paper surface. Use spacing and rules instead of decorative cards. Capture status, errors and Stop remain visible. The active question appears in a dedicated companion area or a separate desktop window.
- Show one complete cue on a pale sage card with dark ink, lighter than the evergreen navigation so it reads as a note beside the conversation, not an alert. Beneath the cue, one muted source line names where it comes from (a prior note and its date, or this conversation); that line is the control that opens the excerpts and keeps the cue for reading. There is no separate Why? button. A corner Dismiss is always available. An untouched cue softens over its last four seconds before it leaves; there is no countdown or progress bar. Cues support the current conversation; there is no Save action or saved-question workflow. Context can collapse without losing the question. Held questions remain actionable while paused and after playback finishes; label them as earlier when their original time window passes. Untouched questions expire quietly. There is no visible countdown, scrolling script, or response score. See [Glance view evidence](docs/glance-mode.md).
- The desktop companion defaults to the bottom-right of the notebook display, with optional top-center, top-left, and top-right presets in Settings. Its resting bar is 320 × 76 logical pixels. A question uses 280 of height and a width that follows the work area: 360 on one narrower than 1100 logical pixels, 480 on one wider than 2400, 420 between. Revealing sources adds 140 of height, and an answer another 100. Larger text adds 90 pixels of height. All sizes are clamped to the work area, with scrolling when needed. Bottom placement keeps its bottom-right corner; top presets keep their top edge. A paused question stays visible. The bar returns on dismissal or untouched expiry.
- Use 16px control corners and 24–28px panel/question corners, with consistent stroke icons. The native window remains opaque. On macOS, AppKit supplies the rounded outer frame around full-size content; the title and window buttons are hidden using public APIs. Other platforms retain a borderless window.
- Primary actions are at least 48px high and icon controls at least 44px in each dimension. Keep a clear focus ring on both paper and forest surfaces. Separators organize content without enclosing every paragraph in a card.
- Hover, focus, loading, error, empty, paused, and ended states are part of the interface. A new question expands once over 240ms from just below full size; opening its source reveals the excerpt over 180ms. Timer updates do not repeat the entrance, and closing or dismissing is immediate. Reduced motion removes these animations and control transitions while preserving all content and actions. No bounce, pulsing, or celebratory animation competes with the meeting. Native window resizing remains immediate; the animation belongs to the rendered question.
- All sample content is labeled demo. No fake capture status, model readiness, integrations, or persistence guarantees.
