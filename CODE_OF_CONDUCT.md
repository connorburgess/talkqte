# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome. Critique the work, explain your reasoning, and leave room for others to learn.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing another person's private information, and repeated hostile behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed. Apply these standards consistently, including to maintainers.

For a concern involving private information, contact the repository owner through a private contact method they publish on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without naming people or describing sensitive details. Do not post private incident reports publicly.
